The Home page (Home, Overview tab) is a one-screen summary of the pipeline for the as-of date. It has no period selector of its own: every figure is calculated for the date shown in the header, so moving the date with the season bar or the date picker changes the whole page.

## KPI row

Each KPI shows a value for the as-of date and a change against an earlier period.

| KPI | What it measures | Compared with |
|---|---|---|
| Open Pipeline | Total amount of deals open on the as-of date | 30 days earlier |
| Weighted Pipeline | Open amount weighted by probability | 30 days earlier |
| Closed Won (current quarter) | Won amount from the start of the quarter to the as-of date | The same number of days into the previous quarter |
| Win Rate (12 mo) | Won deals divided by decided deals over the trailing 12 months | The 12 months before that |
| Average Deal Size | Average won amount over the trailing 12 months | The 12 months before that |

## Focus this month

### Top Commodity

Shows the crop to focus on now, its phase (for example post-harvest) and the regions where most of its accounts share that phase, with a one-line reason and a count of accounts out of blackout.

Each crop is scored by the estimated deal value of its accounts (prospect score times estimated deal, discounted for blackouts), weighted by crop phase:

| Phase | Weight |
|---|---|
| Post-harvest | 1.25 |
| Off-season | 1.1 |
| Growing | 1.0 |
| Planting | 0.6 |
| Harvest | 0.4 |

Phases come from the crop calendar. See [Seasonality Rules](/help/seasonality-rules).

### Top Region

Shows the region with the most estimated deal value (the same value without the phase weight), the number of accounts out of blackout and the open pipeline. Click **View on map** to open the Map drilled into that region.

## Charts

- **Pipeline by Stage**: open amount and deal count for each stage. Hover a bar to see the figures.
- **Closed Won by Month**: won amount for each of the last 12 months, ending with the month of the as-of date. Hover a bar to see the month, amount and deal count.

The charts are for reading only. Clicking a bar does not filter the page.

## Top 10 Open Opportunities

The ten open deals with the highest expected value (close rate for the deal's creation month times amount). Columns: Account, Segment, Region, Amount, Stage, Close and Blackout.

The Blackout column shows:

- "To" and a date when the account is in a harvest no-contact period.
- "Planting" when it is in the light planting period.
- "Close date" when the close date falls inside a blackout.
- "Open" otherwise.

Click an account name to open the opportunity. In mock mode this opens the in-app record; in live mode it opens the record in Salesforce Lightning. Click **View all** to go to Segments, which lists the top 25 open deals.

## Upcoming

Up to five dated items in the next 120 days, with at most two of each kind:

- **Harvest blackout ends** for a region, with the number of accounts. Click to open the Map on that region.
- **Selling window opens** (for example Year-end and audit season). Click to open the Calendar.
- **Board meeting** at an account with an open deal. Click to open the account.
- **Close date** of an open opportunity. Click to open the opportunity.

If nothing falls in the next 120 days, the card says so.

## Tips

- Set the date to **October: harvest** and then **December: year-end** to see Focus this month and the blackout flags change.
- The segment ranking is not on Home. Open the **Segments** tab for it. See [Segment Prioritization](/help/segment-prioritization).
