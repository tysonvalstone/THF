The New Builds Finder is not available in this version of HarvestSignal yet. The app does not scan for new grain or feed facility construction, and the Map has no new-builds layer. The closest tools today help you find areas and facilities where ThiboLiSoft has no customer yet.

## What you can do today

### Find whitespace counties

A whitespace county is a county that has target facilities but no ThiboLiSoft customer.

1. Go to **Map**, then the **Facilities** tab.
2. Choose an area: Illinois + Iowa, Illinois, Iowa, or All US and Canada.
3. Review the coverage figures (for example "12 of 132 Iowa co-op locations") and the **Whitespace Counties** list.
4. Filter the facility table by segment or status (customers or prospects), or search by name, county or railroad.
5. Export the list as a Salesforce-ready CSV if you want to load it elsewhere. See [Exports & Templates](/help/exports-templates).

### Browse prospects on the Map

1. Go to **Map** and drill into a region or state.
2. In the sidebar **Prospects** tab, set **Status** to Prospects and filter by commodity, size and segment.
3. Sort by **Score** or **Est. deal size**.
4. Click **Enroll top 25 in sequence** to start outreach to the list.

The **Area** tab shows penetration (customers as a share of facilities) and the top potential customers for the area.

### Plan visits to uncovered areas

The Trip Planner gives a +6 bonus to facilities in a whitespace county, so trips lean toward areas with no customer yet. See [Trip Planner](/help/trip-planner).

### Add facilities you already know about

In mock mode you can import a CSV of accounts on the Facilities tab. New accounts are added and existing ones updated. Imported data is stored in your browser only. In live mode, add the accounts in Salesforce and click **Refresh data**.

## Planned

The following is planned and not built yet:

- Scan public sources for new grain elevator, co-op, ethanol, feed mill and processor construction and expansions.
- A managed list of sources to scan (for example permits, trade press and company announcements).
- Show new builds on the Map with the stage of construction.
- Convert a result into a lead with one click, so it can be scored and added to campaigns and sequences.
