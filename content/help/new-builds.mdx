New Builds (Map section, New Builds tab) lists grain, ethanol, feed and processing facilities that have been announced, are in permitting, are under construction or are commissioning. A new facility has no software vendor yet, so it is one of the best times to start a conversation. Each project shows the company, town, facility type and segment, capacity, estimated investment, expected completion date and the public source it came from.

## Browse and filter

1. Go to **Map**, then **New Builds**.
2. The list shows projects with status **New** by default. Use the filters above the list to narrow it:
   - **Status**: New, Converted (already a lead), Dismissed, or all statuses.
   - **Stage**: Announced, Permitting, Under Construction or Commissioning.
   - **Segment**: for example Ethanol Plant, Feed Mill or Rail/Shuttle Loader.
   - **Region** and **State or province**.
3. Search by project, company, town or source.
4. Click a column heading to sort, for example by investment or expected completion. The list shows 10 projects per page; change **Rows** at the bottom to see 25 or 50.

The map next to the list shows each project as a dot coloured by stage. Choosing a region or state zooms the map to it. Click a dot to open that project.

## Scan sources

Click **Scan sources** to check the public sources for new projects. The scan walks through each source in turn and shows what it found:

- State air permit applications
- County board minutes
- Trade press
- USDA grant announcements
- Economic development releases
- Rail spur filings

A scan usually finds one to three projects, which are added to the list with status New. Click **View** next to a result to open it. Scanning again on the same date finds nothing new. Change the as-of date in the header to see what a scan on another day would find.

In this version the scan is simulated: it does not contact any website, and the projects it finds are generated sample data.

## Work a project

Click a project to open its details: the summary, stage, segment, capacity, estimated investment, announced date, expected completion and the source.

- **Convert to lead** creates a lead with the company, town, state, facility type, segment, region and capacity filled in, lead source Web and status Open - Not Contacted. The project is marked Converted and linked to the lead, and a call task is added for the lead's owner. The lead then appears in Prospects with a score.
- **Open lead** goes to the lead once the project is converted.
- **Dismiss** hides a project that is not a fit. It moves to the Dismissed status, where **Restore** brings it back.

Convert and Dismiss show an **Undo** button for a few seconds.

## Convert the lead to an account

When you have talked to the company:

1. Open the lead, then click **Convert to account**.
2. Check the account name.
3. Choose whether to create a contact from the person on the lead. New-build leads start without a named person, so add one with **Edit** first if you have a name.
4. Optionally tick **Create an opportunity** and set its name and close date. The amount is estimated from the products that fit the facility type.
5. Click **Convert**.

The account opens. The lead is marked converted and links to the account, and its tasks and campaign memberships move to the account.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the projects that match your filters. Choose and order the columns in the column picker, and save the setup as a preset. See [Exports & Templates](/help/exports-templates).

## Local changes

New projects, conversions and dismissals are stored in your browser as local changes. When HarvestSignal is connected to live Salesforce they are not written back, and changed records show a **Local change, not synced** tag.
