The Campaigns section has three tabs. **Campaigns** builds a one-off campaign for a selling-window play. **Sequences** runs multi-step outreach (email, call and LinkedIn tasks) for chosen contacts. **Calendar** shows the selling windows through the year and the campaign planned for each. Nothing is actually sent: in mock mode the app logs Salesforce-style records in your browser, and in live mode it writes nothing.

## Build a campaign

1. Go to **Campaigns** and click **New Campaign**.
2. **Audience**: choose a season play (Year-end, Implementation, Budget window, Quick wins, Harvest support or Year-round), regions, facility types and an optional commodity. The play suggested for the as-of date is selected first.
3. **Targets**: set **Min score** (default 55) and untick any accounts you want to leave out. Targets are ranked by prospect score.
4. **Content**: use the built-in template, or click **Write with AI** when an AI key is configured. You can edit the copy.
5. **Launch**: set the campaign name, type (Direct Mail, Multi-Channel, Email or Call Blitz) and start date. The summary shows members, average score, estimated budget, expected responses, expected pipeline and return on budget.
6. Click **Export mail list (CSV)** to download the recipients, or launch the campaign.

Launching creates a Campaign, a CampaignMember for each recipient and a follow-up task for each member (mock mode). The campaign page shows dates, members, responses, budget, expected pipeline, owner and content, and you can export its mail list again.

Mail-list CSV exports open the column picker. See [Exports & Templates](/help/exports-templates).

## Use the Calendar

The **Calendar** tab lists the selling windows and a planned campaign for each. The Year-end window starts with the **Price-Later Contract Compliance Webinar** (invite copy, a controller and GM target list and a follow-up sequence). Click **Plan the webinar**, or the plan button for another window, to open the campaign builder pre-filled. The tab also shows the crop calendar by region.

## Sequences

A sequence is a list of steps, each with a type (**Email**, **Call** task or **LinkedIn** task) and a day offset. Four sequences are prebuilt: Post-Harvest Co-op Outreach, Year-Round Ethanol & Feed, Price-Later Compliance Webinar and Visiting Next Week.

### Create or edit a sequence

1. Open **Campaigns**, then the **Sequences** tab.
2. Select a sequence, or click **New sequence**. To have the AI write one, click **Draft sequence** and describe it (segment, month or season phase).
3. Add, remove or reorder steps, and set each step's day.
4. Write the subject and body. Use **Insert field** to add merge fields.
5. Click **Rewrite** on a step and choose Shorter, Friendlier, More formal or Clearer call to action (AI key required).
6. Click **Save**. You must save changes before you can enroll anyone.

Prebuilt sequences can be hidden but not deleted. Sequences you create are stored in your browser.

### Merge fields

Available fields: `{{contact.first_name}}`, `{{contact.title}}`, `{{account.name}}`, `{{account.locations}}`, `{{region}}`, `{{state}}`, `{{commodity}}`, `{{season_phase}}`, `{{blackout_end_date}}`, `{{fiscal_year_end}}`, `{{next_board_meeting}}` and `{{sender.name}}`.

The season phase is worked out for each step's send date. Open **Preview** and pick any recipient to see the rendered text. Missing values are highlighted in amber, and a recipient with any missing field can't be enrolled until you change the text or the recipient.

### Variants

A step can have variants, each with rules on **Season phase**, **Commodity**, **Region**, **State** or **Segment**. For each recipient the first variant whose rules all match is used; otherwise the default text is used.

### Scheduling around blackouts

- In a harvest (hard) blackout, every step moves to the first allowed day.
- In a planting (light) blackout, only call steps move.
- Weekend dates move to Monday.
- Later steps keep their spacing after a move.

### Enroll recipients

1. Select a saved sequence and click **Enroll**.
2. Filter contacts by region, state, segment, facility type, commodity, and customers or prospects, or search.
3. Tick recipients. Each shows a status (for example Ready, No contact or Already enrolled) and the variant it will get.
4. Confirm. Steps are logged as scheduled Salesforce tasks in mock mode. In live mode no tasks are created.

You can also start enrollment from the Map (**Enroll top 25 in sequence**), the Trip Planner (**Enroll in sequence**) or the assistant (**Start sequence**).
