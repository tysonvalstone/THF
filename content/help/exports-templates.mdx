You can export data from HarvestSignal in two ways. Most lists have an **Export CSV** button that opens a column picker. For reports you run again and again, the **Export Template Builder** (Templates, Exports tab) lets you save a template with its own columns, filters, grouping and format.

## Choose columns for a CSV export

Every CSV export opens the same dialog. It applies to:

- The Prospects list (Home, Prospects).
- Salesforce-ready accounts on the Facilities tab (the **Export** button).
- Campaign mail lists, in the campaign builder and on a campaign page.
- Trip itineraries from the Trip Planner.
- CSV exports from the Export Template Builder, including the Salesforce Opportunity Import template.

To set up the file:

1. Click **Export CSV** (or **Export** on Facilities).
2. Tick or untick columns to show or hide them. Use **Select all** or **Clear all** to change them all at once.
3. Drag a column by its handle to reorder it, or use the move up and move down buttons.
4. Type in a column's header box to rename it in the file.
5. Check the live preview of the first 5 rows.
6. Under **Rows**, choose **Current view** (only the rows that match the page's filters) or **All rows**, where the page has filters.
7. Under **Date format**, choose YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY.
8. Click the export button in the dialog to download the file.

### Salesforce-ready exports

In exports meant for Salesforce import, the required import fields are locked on and show a lock icon. You can't hide or rename them. Optional fields can be hidden, renamed and reordered as usual.

### Presets

- To reuse a setup, type a name in the preset box and save it. Choose it from **Preset** next time, or delete it with the delete button.
- The last setup you used for each export is remembered, so the dialog opens the way you left it.

Presets and last-used setups are stored in your browser. They are not shared with other users or devices.

## Build an export template

1. Go to **Templates**, then **Exports**.
2. Select a template in the list, or click **New template**.
3. To start from a description, type what you want (for example "Board summary of Illinois pipeline by segment with win rates") and click **Generate**. With an AI key, the AI turns it into a template, which is checked against the list of available fields before it is shown. Without a key, the button reads **Build** and picks the closest prebuilt template, adding any state, region or segment filters it recognises.
4. Edit the template:
   - **Data source**: Opportunities, Accounts, Contacts or Segments.
   - **Columns**: add, remove and reorder.
   - **Filters**: for example state in IL, IA.
   - **Group by**, **Sort** and **Totals**.
   - **Format**: CSV, XLSX or PDF.
5. Check the preview, which shows the first 20 rows with any subtotals and totals.
6. Click **Save template**. Use **Duplicate** to copy a template and **Delete** to remove one (prebuilt templates are hidden rather than deleted).
7. Click **Export CSV**, **Export XLSX** or **Export PDF** (the label follows the format). CSV exports open the column picker described above.

Percent values export as 0 to 100 in CSV and as formatted percentages in XLSX and PDF. Templates you create are stored in your browser.

## Prebuilt templates

| Template | Source | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| Pipeline by Segment | Opportunities | Open opportunities grouped by segment with seasonal win rates and expected value |
| Territory Coverage | Accounts | Every facility by state and county, with customer coverage and open pipeline |
| Accounts in Blackout | Accounts | Accounts in a harvest or planting no-contact window, soonest to clear first |
| Salesforce Opportunity Import | Opportunities | Open opportunities with Salesforce API field names as headers, ready for Data Loader |

The Salesforce Opportunity Import template includes Name, AccountId, StageName, Amount, CloseDate, Probability, Type, LeadSource, OwnerId, NextStep and Economic_Buyer_Identified__c, sorted by close date.

## Export from the assistant

When the assistant answers with a table, click **Export this** to open the answer as a template in the builder. See [AI Assistant](/help/ai-assistant).
