HarvestSignal helps ThiboLiSoft's sales team decide who to call and when. It reads opportunity history, works out which market segments close best for deals started this month, and flags accounts that are in a harvest or planting no-contact period. This article covers signing in, navigation, the as-of date and where to get help.

## Sign in

1. Open the app and enter your work email and password.
2. If an administrator gave you a temporary password, you are asked to choose your own before you continue.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email.

You stay signed in as you move between pages, refresh or reopen the browser. To end the session, click your avatar in the top right and choose **Sign out**.

Accounts are created by an administrator; there is no self sign-up. To change your name, title or password, open **Settings** from the avatar menu.

### Administrators

Administrators see **Users** in the avatar menu and under **Settings**. From there you can:

1. **Add user:** enter their name, email, title and role, link them to a Salesforce user (so records they create have the right owner), and choose how they get access: a temporary password you share with them, or an invitation email.
2. **Edit** a user's name, title, role or Salesforce user.
3. **Reset a password** by emailing a reset link or setting a new temporary password.
4. **Disable** an account to block sign-in without deleting it, or **Delete** it.

You can't remove your own administrator role, disable or delete yourself, or remove the last administrator.

When the app is first set up, the sign-in page asks for the first administrator's details instead.

In demo deployments without an account service, the sign-in page shows **Select user** with the four demo users instead.

Records you create in the app (tasks, events, campaigns) are owned by the signed-in user, and you are the sender on generated emails.

## Find your way around

The top navigation has four sections. Each section has tabs just below it.

| Section | Tabs | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Overview, Segments, Prospects | Dashboard, segment ranking, scored prospect list |
| Map | Map, Facilities | Seasonality map, recent sales, trip planning, facility coverage |
| Campaigns | Campaigns, Sequences, Calendar | Build campaigns, run email sequences, see the selling calendar |
| Templates | Exports | Build and run export templates |

Account, lead and opportunity pages, and **Settings**, sit under Home.

The header also shows:

- A **Mock data** or **Live Salesforce** badge, which tells you where the data comes from. Hover it to see when the data was loaded and how many warnings there are.
- **Refresh data**, which reloads the data (in live mode it queries Salesforce again).
- The **as-of date** button.
- A **?** icon that opens the Help Center.
- Your avatar, with Settings, Reset local changes and Sign out.

## Change the as-of date (time travel)

Most of HarvestSignal depends on the date: close rates for the current month, segment rankings, blackouts, the map colours and the campaign plays. You can move the date to see how the plan changes through the year.

- Use the **season bar** under the navigation: step back or forward one week, press **Play** to run through the year, or click or drag the timeline. The track is coloured by grain selling window.
- Click the date button to pick an exact date (between 2024 and 2027) or a preset: October: harvest, December: year-end, March: live before planting, July: budget window, Early August: quick wins.
- Click **Today** in the date picker to return to the real date.

When you are away from today, the date button reads "As of" followed by the date.

## Mock data and live Salesforce

By default the app runs on generated mock data, and changes you make (emails, calls, campaigns, CSV imports) are stored only in your browser. Choose **Reset local changes** in the avatar menu to discard them. When an administrator connects a Salesforce org, the badge changes to **Live Salesforce** and the app becomes read-only. See [Salesforce Connection (Admin)](/help/salesforce-connection).

## Demo Mode

Demo Mode runs the whole story on mock data: a new ethanol plant becomes a customer, and the app follows it from lead to renewal. Everything happens through the same buttons and records as normal use, so every page reflects each step.

**Turn it on.** Open the avatar menu and switch on **Demo Mode**. Guests are always in Demo Mode: open `/?guest=1` (or `/demo`) to sign in as a guest, with the Sales manager role and **View as** in the avatar menu.

**The Demo Mode bar** sits under the header while it's on:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start demo** | Starts the guided walkthrough (or **Resume demo** where you left it) |
| **Fast-forward 7 days** / **30 days** | Moves the as-of date forward and simulates what happens meanwhile: sequence emails go out, opens, replies and meetings, a few stage moves, wins and losses, and invoice payments. One message sums it up. |
| **Activity** | Everything that happened in the demo, newest first, with links to the records |
| **Reset Demo** | After you confirm, discards every demo change, puts the date back to Sep 29, 2026 and clears the walkthrough |

**The walkthrough** has 13 steps. The panel at the bottom of the page shows the step and a one-line caption. Use **Next** and **Back**, **Auto-play** to advance on its own, or close it with the **X**. Each step does the real work (creates the records, moves the date, switches role where the story needs it) and then opens the page that shows the result:

1. October 14: ethanol plants and feed mills rank first during harvest.
2. The new Fort Dodge ethanol plant becomes a lead, then an account with its plant controller.
3. An opportunity sized from product fit; open pipeline goes up.
4. The Year-Round Ethanol and Feed campaign, with 15 contacts enrolled in the prebuilt sequence.
5. A discovery call on the Call Desk: brief, simulated transcript, AI Notes, stage change and follow-up task.
6. A quote from the catalog at 12% off (auto-approved), marked Sent.
7. Fast-forward 30 days: campaign engagement, the quote accepted and the deal Closed Won.
8. The contract: Legal approves a non-standard clause (the app views as Legal for that moment), the customer signs, and the first invoice goes out.
9. Finance: ARR goes up and the ARR bridge shows the new customer.
10. December 10: co-ops move to the top as the harvest blackout ends.
11. The trip planner: "I'm going to Iowa in January, top 10 co-ops."
12. Eleven months later: the renewal opportunity opens by itself and customer health flags an expansion.
13. The Board Report PDF downloads. Demo complete.

Going back and forward again never creates duplicates. The demo keeps its own change log, separate from your other local changes.

## Get help

- Click the **?** icon in the header to open the Help Center at `/help`. Use the table of contents on the left, or search across article titles and text.
- Most pages have a small **?** link that opens the matching article in a drawer on the right, so you can read it without leaving the page.
- Ask the assistant (button at the bottom right of every page). It can search these articles for "how do I" questions. See [AI Assistant](/help/ai-assistant).
