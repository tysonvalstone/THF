Call Desk lives under **Call Desk** in the top navigation, with two tabs: **Today** and **Past calls**. It gives you a short brief before each call and turns the call itself into a transcript and structured notes, called **AI Notes**. The notes feed the next brief for that account.

Reps see their own calls. Managers can switch between **My calls** and **Team**.

## Today's calls

The left column is the agenda for the app date (time travel changes it). Use the arrows or the date box to look at another day, and **Today** to come back.

Each call shows the time and length, the account, the contacts on the invite, the call type (Discovery, Demo, Follow-up, Negotiation, Renewal or Check-in), the linked opportunity and a status:

- **Brief ready**: an upcoming call.
- **Next**: the next call is outlined, with a countdown such as "in 12 min". The countdown uses the app date at the current time of day.
- **Notes saved** or **Notes pending**: calls that already happened.

Click a call to open its brief on the right. The next call opens by default. On a phone the brief appears under the agenda.

## Schedule a call

1. Click **Schedule call** and pick the account.
2. Choose the call type, opportunity, date, time, length and the contacts to invite. Managers also pick the rep.
3. Click **Schedule**.

You can also schedule from an account page, an opportunity page, the map drill-down list, a Trip Planner stop (a confirmation call the weekday before the visit) and a call step in a sequence preview. Each has a small **Schedule call** button.

To change or cancel a call, open it and click **Edit**. **Delete** asks for confirmation, and you can undo it for a few seconds.

## The pre-call brief

The brief is built to be read in about 60 seconds. The blocks are, in order:

1. **Snapshot**: account, segment, locations, main commodity and region, the season status (for example "In harvest blackout until Nov 26", or "Year-round" for ethanol plants, feed mills and processors), customer health, open pipeline, the active quote and contract, and overdue invoices.
2. **Who's on the call**: each contact's title and buying role, when you last spoke, and gaps such as "Economic buyer not identified".
3. **Last time**: a three-line summary of the most recent call, the commitments on both sides (overdue ones in amber) and the questions that were answered.
4. **Interaction timeline**: calls with their AI summaries, emails and sequence steps, meetings, quotes, contract events, support tickets and invoices, newest first. Filter by type; 10 per page.
5. **Questions to ask**: five to seven questions tailored to the segment, deal stage, season, missing information and what was said last time. Check them off as you ask them; the checks are saved with the call.
6. **Talking points**: products that fit the facility, a nearby reference customer (a recent win in the same segment, same region when possible) and likely objections with short answers.
7. **Risks**: a close date inside a blackout or before the next board meeting, a missing economic buyer, a competitor from the last call, falling health, overdue invoices and quotes about to expire.

**Copy brief** puts the brief on the clipboard as plain text. **PDF** downloads a branded one-page brief.

When an AI key is set, the assistant writes the at-a-glance summary, the questions, the talking points and the objections from the account's data, and the brief is tagged **AI brief**. Without a key, the rules version fills every block and is tagged **Rules brief**.

## Take the call with AI Notes

1. Click **Start call**. The call opens in a focused view.
2. Pick the transcription:
   - **Simulated transcript** (the default, and the only option in Demo Mode) plays a realistic conversation for this call type and segment, line by line, with the rep and contact names.
   - **Microphone** uses the browser's speech recognition where it's supported. Tell everyone on the call that it's being recorded and transcribed, and confirm that before you start. A red **Recording** indicator shows while it runs.
3. During the call you see the questions to check off, the brief (collapsed), the live transcript, your own notes and a timer. **Skip ahead** fills in the rest of a simulated transcript.
4. Click **End call**.

## Review and save AI Notes

After the call, AI Notes combines the transcript and your rough notes into:

- **Summary** (three or four lines), **Key points** and **Pain points**
- **Objections raised** and how you handled them
- **Qualification**: budget, decision maker or economic buyer, timeline, competitors and number of locations
- **Next steps**: action items with an owner (you or the customer) and a due date
- **Sentiment**: Positive, Neutral or Concerned
- **Suggested CRM updates**, such as the next stage, a new close date, the economic buyer or the location count
- A **follow-up email** draft filled in with the same merge fields as sequences

Everything is editable. Nothing in **Suggested CRM updates** is applied until you check it. A deal can't leave Prospecting until the economic buyer is identified, so a stage change that needs it stays disabled until you also confirm the economic buyer.

**Send via Outreach** logs the follow-up email on the account with a follow-up task, the same way the Email button does.

**Save to account** marks the call completed with its transcript, your notes, the AI Notes and the commitments, creates a task for each next step and applies the updates you confirmed. Every change is recorded in the audit log. With live Salesforce, changes stay in the app and are tagged **Local change, not synced**.

The next brief for that account shows these notes, the open commitments and the questions you answered. To change or remove notes later, open the call and use **Edit** or **Delete** on the AI Notes.

## Past calls

**Call Desk → Past calls** lists completed calls, 10 to a page. Filter by account, call type, sentiment (or notes pending) and date range; managers can switch to **Team** and filter by rep. The search box looks through transcripts and notes, so "scale tickets" or "competitor" finds the calls where it came up and shows the matching line.

Click a call to read its notes, commitments, questions asked, the actions taken when the notes were saved, and the full transcript.

You can also ask the assistant, for example "What did Cedar Bend Cooperative say about budget last month?". It searches the same call notes, with or without an AI key.
