## Dates and data

### Why do the numbers change when I change the date?

Almost everything is calculated for the as-of date in the header: close rates for the current month, rankings, blackouts, Home KPIs, map colours and campaign plays. Click **Today** in the date picker to return to the real date. See [Getting Started](/help/getting-started).

### Is this real customer data?

When the header badge says **Mock data**, no. The data is generated, and company and people names are fictional. When it says **Live Salesforce**, the app is reading your org. See [Salesforce Connection (Admin)](/help/salesforce-connection).

### Where did my emails, campaigns or imports go?

In mock mode, changes are stored in your browser only. They do not appear in another browser or device, or in a private window, and clearing site data removes them. **Reset local changes** in the avatar menu also removes them.

### Why can't I send emails or log calls?

In live mode the app is read-only, so outreach actions are disabled. Nothing is ever actually sent in either mode: emails and sequence steps are logged as tasks in mock mode.

### How current is live data?

Live data is cached for 1 hour. Click **Refresh data** in the header to query Salesforce again.

## Blackouts and seasons

### Why does the app tell me not to contact an account?

Elevators, co-ops, river terminals, shuttle loaders and seed cleaners are in a harvest no-contact period from mid-August to Thanksgiving, and a light no-contact period during spring planting. Dates move later the further north the account is. See [Seasonality Rules](/help/seasonality-rules).

### Why is an ethanol plant never in blackout?

Ethanol plants, feed mills and processors run year-round, so they have no blackout. They tend to rank highest during harvest.

### Are the map colours actual crop progress?

No. They come from a model based on USDA NASS usual planting and harvesting dates, shifted by latitude. See [Map](/help/map).

## Rankings and scores

### What does "Not enough data" mean on a segment?

The segment has fewer than 10 decided deals for that period, so the app does not show a bare percentage. The rate used for ranking is blended toward the company rate. See [Segment Prioritization](/help/segment-prioritization).

### What do Measured, Blended and Prior mean?

**Measured** is based on 10 or more decided deals. **Blended** mixes a thin segment's own results with the company rate. **Prior** uses the company rate because the segment has no history.

### Why did a segment jump when I moved a slider?

The weights change the priority score immediately. Click **Reset** to go back to the defaults.

### How is a prospect's score calculated?

It adds six factors: season timing (30), fit and size (20), market signal (15), displacement (15), engagement (15) and climate (5). 72 or more is Hot and 60 to 71 is Warm.

## Map and trips

### Why doesn't the Trip Planner pick the highest-scored accounts?

It balances score against distance (one point per 4 miles) to keep the trip compact, and reduces the score of accounts in a blackout on the travel date. See [Trip Planner](/help/trip-planner).

### Are drive times accurate?

They are estimates: straight-line miles times 1.3, at 55 mph. Check a routing app before you travel.

### Why is the products list empty on a recent sale?

In live mode the app does not read opportunity line items from Salesforce.

## Exports

### How do I export only some columns?

Click **Export CSV**, untick the columns you don't need and reorder or rename the rest. Save the setup as a preset to reuse it. See [Exports & Templates](/help/exports-templates).

### Why can't I hide a column?

In Salesforce-ready exports, required import fields are locked on (shown with a lock icon) so the file can be imported.

### My presets are missing.

Presets and templates you create are stored in your browser. They are not available in another browser or on another device.

## Assistant

### The assistant says "AI offline — limited answers".

The server has no Anthropic API key. The assistant still answers common questions from the data and links Help Center articles. See [AI Assistant](/help/ai-assistant).

### Why doesn't the assistant see a campaign I just created?

The assistant reads the server copy of the data. Changes made in mock mode are stored only in your browser.

## Sign-in

### How long does sign-in last?

30 days. Only **Sign out** in the avatar menu ends the session earlier.

### I forgot my password.

Profiles and passwords are stored in the browser you set them in. Clearing that browser's site data for HarvestSignal removes your profile and password, along with any mock-mode changes. Production deployments should use Salesforce SSO or the company identity provider instead.

## Salesforce connection

### Salesforce login fails with invalid_grant or INVALID_LOGIN.

For `invalid_grant`, turn on Allow OAuth Username-Password Flows and check that the user may use the Connected App. For `INVALID_LOGIN`, check the username and password, append the security token and make sure `SF_LOGIN_URL` points at the right environment. If the badge still says Mock data, one of the five `SF_` variables is missing or the app was not redeployed. See [Salesforce Connection (Admin)](/help/salesforce-connection).

## Features not available yet

### Can I build a quote, manage products or find new facility construction?

Not yet. See [Quotes](/help/quotes), [Product Setup](/help/product-setup) and [New Builds Finder](/help/new-builds) for what you can do today and what is planned.
