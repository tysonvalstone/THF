The catalog is managed under **Quoting → Products** and **Quoting → Price books**. **Settings → Products** opens the same page. Quotes and the opportunities created from outreach read the catalog, so a change here applies to new work right away.

Changes are saved as Salesforce-style Product2, ProductType, Pricebook2 and PricebookEntry records. In live Salesforce mode they stay in the app and are tagged **Local change, not synced**.

## Products

The **Products** table lists each product's name and description, code, type, pricing unit, list price (from the standard price book), status, best-fit facility types and how many quote lines use it. Filter by type or status, search by name or code, and export to CSV.

### Add or edit a product

1. Click **New product**, or click a row to edit it.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** and **Product code** (codes must be unique).
   - **Type**: one of the product types below.
   - **Pricing unit**: **per year** (one subscription), **per location / year** (quantity defaults to the account's locations) or **one-time**.
   - **Unit label** (optional): priced per unit, for example kiosk. Kiosk quantities default to the account's truck scales; a label of location defaults to its locations.
   - **List price**: saved as the product's price in the standard price book.
   - **Active**: inactive products can't be added to new quotes.
   - **Best fit**: the facility types the product suits. New quotes without opportunity products use these to suggest up to three modules plus implementation.
3. Click **Save**. A new product is added to the standard price book at its list price. Add it to other price books under **Price books**.

### Delete or deactivate

Delete asks for confirmation, removes the product's prices from every price book, and can be undone for a few seconds. A product used on any quote or opportunity line can't be deleted; you are offered **Deactivate** instead, which keeps existing quotes intact and hides it from new ones.

## Product types

The **Product types** table (on the same page) lists each type's sort order, name, description, number of products and status. Types group products in the catalog and on the Add product list.

- **New type** adds one; click a row to edit its name, sort order, description or status.
- A type used by products can't be deleted; deactivate it, or move its products to another type first.

## Price books

**Quoting → Price books** lists each price book with its currency, number of products, number of quotes using it and status. Click a row to show its prices below.

The starting price books:

- **Standard Price Book** (USD): list prices.
- **Multi-Location Co-op** (USD): per-location modules 15% below list, the default for co-ops with 5 or more locations.
- **Canada (CAD)**: list prices in Canadian dollars, the default for Canadian accounts.

### Maintain prices

- Edit a price in the **Price** column and press Enter. The **vs. standard** column shows the difference from the standard price. Editing a price in the standard book also updates the product's list price.
- Untick **Active** to keep a price but hide the product from new quotes on that book.
- **Add product** adds a product that isn't in the book yet, at its standard price.
- The bin removes a product from the book (with Undo). Existing quotes keep their lines.
- **From Standard ±%** (non-standard books) resets every price to the standard price plus or minus a percentage, rounded to $10, and adds missing products. Use -15 for a 15% discount book, or +36 for a rough CAD conversion.

Changing prices never changes existing quotes. To re-price a draft quote, change its price book in **Edit header**.

### Create, edit or delete a price book

1. Click **New price book**.
2. Enter a name, currency and description. Leave **Copy prices from Standard Price Book** ticked to start from standard prices, and set an **Adjustment** percentage if you like.
3. Click **Create price book**.

Click **Edit** on a row to rename it, change its currency or description, or deactivate it. Inactive price books aren't offered on new quotes. The standard price book can't be deactivated or deleted. A price book used by quotes can't be deleted; you are offered **Deactivate** instead.
