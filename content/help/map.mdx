The Map (Map section, Map tab) shows US states and Canadian provinces coloured by where a crop is in its season on the as-of date, with your prospects and customers as dots. Use it to see which areas are in harvest, drill down to a state, check recent wins and build a list of accounts to contact or visit.

## Choose what the map shows

1. Pick a view in the toolbar:
   - **Season** colours each area by crop phase for the crop you choose (Wheat, Corn, Soybeans, Rice or Lentils). Blue means planting and red means harvest peak. The colour varies within a state by latitude, so southern areas turn red earlier than northern ones.
   - **Commodities** tints each state by its dominant crop and colours each dot by the account's primary commodity.
2. In the Commodities view, click **Colors** to choose your own colour for each commodity. Your choice is saved in your browser. Click **Reset colors** to go back to the defaults.
3. Change the as-of date in the header to see the season move.

The season colours are a model based on USDA NASS usual planting and harvesting dates, shifted by latitude. They are not observed crop-progress data. See [Seasonality Rules](/help/seasonality-rules).

## Drill down

The map has three levels: North America, a sales region (there are 12), and a state or province.

1. Click a state to zoom to its region.
2. Click the state again to zoom to the state.
3. Use the breadcrumb above the map (for example North America, Western Corn Belt, Iowa) to jump back to a level, or click **Back** to go up one level.

You can also scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and use the **+**, **−** and **Fit** buttons. States show an outline when you hover them. At state level, click a facility dot to open the account.

You can link straight to a region or state with `/map?region=` followed by the region id, or `/map?state=IA`.

## Show recent sales

1. Click **Recent sales** in the toolbar.
2. Choose a range: Last 30 days, Last 60 days, Last 90 days or Last 12 months. The range is measured back from the as-of date.
3. Closed-won deals appear as yellow dots sized by amount. A strip above the map shows the number of deals and the total for the range and the current drill-down.
4. Click a yellow dot to see the account, amount, products (line items), close date and owner.

In live mode the app does not read opportunity line items from Salesforce, so the products list is empty.

## Use the sidebar

The sidebar has three tabs.

### Prospects

Filter the accounts shown on the map by **Status** (Prospects, Customers or All), **Commodity**, **Size** (by annual revenue: Large over $100M, Mid $25–100M, Small under $25M) and **Segment**, and sort by **Score**, **Est. deal size** or **Name**. The list and the dots stay in sync: hover a row to highlight its dot, hover a dot to highlight its row, and click a dot to select its row. Up to 600 dots are drawn at once.

Click **Enroll top 25 in sequence** to open Sequences with the top accounts in the list ready to enroll. See [Campaigns & Email Sequences](/help/campaigns-sequences).

### Area

A summary of the current drill-down level: a one-line summary, key stats (facilities, customers, penetration, open pipeline, season phase), top potential customers with estimated deal size and blackout status, **Recent Wins** for the selected range, and three **Key Insights**.

Estimated deal size uses the account's open deal if it has one, otherwise the segment's median won deal.

### Plan a Trip

Builds a multi-day visit itinerary for a state or region. See [Trip Planner](/help/trip-planner).

## Facilities tab

The **Facilities** tab lists target facilities with county, railroad, river and shuttle-loader access and capacity. Choose an area (Illinois + Iowa, Illinois, Iowa, or All US and Canada), see coverage figures such as "12 of 132 Iowa co-op locations", and review **Whitespace Counties**: counties with target facilities but no ThiboLiSoft customer. You can export a Salesforce-ready CSV and, in mock mode, import a CSV. See [Exports & Templates](/help/exports-templates).
